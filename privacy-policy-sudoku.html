Privacy Policy — QuickTap Sudoku

App Name: QuickTap Sudoku
Developer: QuickTap Dev
Last updated: September 2026

QuickTap Sudoku ("the App") is developed by QuickTap Dev. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and who it may be shared with.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account of its own. However, the App optionally integrates with Google Play Games Services to provide sign-in, leaderboards, achievements, and cloud save. If you choose to sign in with your Google account, the following information is processed:

  • Your Google Play Games public profile (display name and player ID), provided by Google Play Games Services.
  • Game progress data such as scores, statistics, streaks, leaderboard rankings, achievements, and daily challenge progress, which is stored using Google's Saved Games (cloud save) feature so it can sync across your devices.

This data is stored by Google Play Games Services under Google's own privacy practices, and is used solely to provide sign-in and cloud save functionality within the App. We do not operate our own servers and do not otherwise collect, store, or sell this information.

Locally Stored Data

Regardless of whether you sign in, the App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Puzzle progress, scores, and statistics
  • Sound and vibration preferences

This local data is not transmitted anywhere unless you sign in to Google Play Games Services, in which case it may be synced as described above.

Playing Without Signing In

Signing in to Google Play Games Services is optional. If you do not sign in, the App works fully offline using only locally stored data, and no account information is collected.

Advertising and Analytics

The App does not display ads and does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Permissions

The App may request device permissions required for standard functionality, such as vibration feedback and network access (used only for Google Play Games Services sign-in and cloud save).

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above, and any account-related data is limited to what Google Play Games Services itself provides when a user chooses to sign in.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
